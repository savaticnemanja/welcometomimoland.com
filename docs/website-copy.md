## Origins

### It began at 30,000 feet, with a pillow and a pen

`NEW` Mimoland did not begin as a business plan. It began in the cabin of an aircraft,
with a distressed child and a crew member who knew how to help.

`NEW` In 2014, Jasna Dragojević drew a face onto an airline pillow to comfort a
distressed child during a flight. The child stopped crying. The pillow became a
character, the character became a story, and the story became a world.

`NEW` That first Mimo — crafted from an in-flight pillow — is still the heart of
everything: the idea that the object a child already holds on a plane can become the
friend that carries them through it.

`NEW` A decade later, Mimoland is a published book, a workshop programme brought to
schools and libraries, a theatre show, a merchandise line and an award-winning
animated film — with the same belief underneath. A child's first flight should inspire
a lifelong love of the sky, not a future fear of flying.

## What Mimoland is

### A structured edutainment model for aviation

One story that travels with the family — before, during and after the flight.

`NEW` **Learning through story, play and emotional connection**

`NEW` Grounded in social-emotional learning and peace education, Mimoland uses
storytelling, guided play and character-led activities to help children recognise
emotions, practise courage and empathy, and approach flying with greater confidence
and curiosity. Mimoland: The First Flight brings this approach to life on screen —
giving children an emotional entry point into a learning journey that continues
through activities, workshops and play.

**Button:** Watch the Film — link to follow

### Pre-Flight

#### Step into the Fairytale

The Mimoland book and home activities spark excitement before the journey. Airport
storytelling and play zones transform waiting time into part of the adventure.

### In-Flight

#### The Story Takes Flight

Princess Stewardesses create an engaging, child-friendly onboard experience.
Edutainment activity packs build confidence. AR pillow companions bring the story to
life in the skies.

### Post-Flight

#### The Adventure Continues

Family shows, workshops and digital experiences extend the connection beyond landing.
They keep the joy of flying alive long after the flight ends.
